# Becoming an AI Product Manager: field guide and 24-week learning path

A 62-page guide (A4) in two formats:

- [PDF](AI-PM-Field-Guide-and-Learning-Path.pdf) for reading and printing
- [Word (.docx)](AI-PM-Field-Guide-and-Learning-Path.docx) for editing and tracking your progress

## What's inside

| Part | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **A · How a modern AI product works** | 16 diagrams and flowcharts. They cover the five-layer AI product stack, how an LLM is built and how it produces an answer, and the life of a single request. Also RAG, agents and MCP, the six workflow and agent patterns, the prompt vs RAG vs fine-tune ladder, the AI-fit check, the eval flywheel, guardrails, model routing and unit economics, and the AI product lifecycle. Each section ends with what the PM decides at that point. |
| **B · What the market asks for** | Analysis of **2,448 AI PM job postings** (1,560 companies, 53 countries) plus a close reading of **44 individual JDs**. The JDs come from frontier labs, big tech, AI startups, Indian product companies (Razorpay, PhonePe, Paytm, Swiggy, Sarvam and others), financial services (Mastercard, Visa, JPMorgan, Amex, Stripe) and consulting firms. Includes 7 charts, 10 cross-JD patterns, a 10-competency model, interview-loop weights and India salary ranges. |
| **C · The 24-week learning path** | Session-by-session plan at 12–15 hours a week, organised in 7 phases. Each week lists its goal, JD evidence, five sessions with step-by-step tasks and named resources, a deliverable, a "done when" test and self-check questions. It produces 5 portfolio projects and a capstone, all in payments and financial services. |
| **Appendices** | Glossary (60+ terms), weekly tracker, AI PRD template, resource library, interview kit (answer structure and technical Q&A), outreach templates, sources. |
