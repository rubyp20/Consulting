1. Purpose and conventions

The taxonomy is the single structure every later deliverable hangs off: as-is maps (1.2) are indexed to it, the activity survey (1.5) uses its L2 categories, controls (1.6) and pain points (1.9) are tagged to it, and the to-be journey patterns in Phase 2 are defined against it. It is agreed once, at WS-01, and then frozen for the engagement.

Conventions

  • L1 and L2 describe what is done, independent of who does it or which deal type triggers it. Deal types and journeys appear at L3.
  • A process appears once in the taxonomy even if many journeys use it. Nine journeys use 1.3 Customer and party identification; it is listed once.
  • Volumes at L2 are 12-month deal counts from the RFP, allocated by the journeys that pass through the process. They are indicative until DR-01 and DR-02 are reconciled at Week 4.
  • “Owner” is the role accountable for the process operating correctly today, not the role that performs most steps.

2. L1 value chain

2.1 Why five stages and not the source’s three

The source maps use three stages per journey: Lead/Customer, Deal & Credit, Completion & Fulfilment. That is a workflow view of a single deal. The taxonomy needs a lifecycle view, because two-thirds of volume is servicing of existing facilities (Renewal 12,938; Variation 7,277) and a further 1,516 deals are distress handling. Stages 4 and 5 make that work visible as its own purpose rather than as repeated origination. The three source stages map onto 1–3 and recur inside 4 and 5, which is the structural finding (F2, F3 in deliverable 1.2) that drives the to-be journey patterns.

What the matrix shows before any workshop

  • Eight live deal types use the same processes 1.3, 1.4, 2.2 and 3.1. The differences are at 1.1–1.2 (new customer or not), 2.1 (full vs. “if required”), 3.2 (security or not) and 3.4 (fulfilment or not).
  • Renewal and EPR, 48.6% of volume, pass through every decision process (2.1–2.4) that New Money does.
  • Three of eleven deal types carry no live customer process (Staged Drawdown, ESR Cleanse, DO NOT USE).
  • The two fast-track types (Shadow Limit Reallocation; Staged Drawdown Allocation) skip 2.1 and reach 2.4 only on exception: the pattern the to-be design generalises.
