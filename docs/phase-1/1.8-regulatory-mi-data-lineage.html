What is and is not known. The RFP states that BLP is a key data source for regulatory reporting and operational MI. It does not say which returns. Section 3 lists the returns a UK business bank would typically source from a lending platform, as a candidate list for Regulatory Reporting to confirm or strike, not as a statement of what the Bank submits. Nothing in this document should be read as asserting the Bank’s regulatory obligations.

1. Purpose and the rule this document enforces

Objective O6 and RFP constraint §7 say the same thing: no design may remove a reporting data point without MI / Regulatory Reporting sign-off. That rule cannot be applied unless someone knows which data points those are. Today, per hypothesis F1, that knowledge sits in extract scripts and in people’s heads. This document makes it explicit in three layers:

LayerQuestion answeredSourceStatus at v0.4
A. Report inventoryWhich regulatory returns and MI reports take data from BLP?DR-10; Reg Reporting; MI ManagerCandidate list; to confirm
B. Field lineageFor each report, which BLP fields, through what transformation, with what manual adjustment?DR-10 extract scripts; MI packsRegister template; populated W4–6
C. Capture-point mappingAt which process step, by whom, is each field created or changed?1.2 maps; 1.6 controlsBuilt from the maps (section 5)

Layer C is the one the redesign actually needs: it tells the Phase 2 team that if a step is eliminated or automated, these fields must still be captured, by some step, to the same definition. The protected-fields list (section 6) is the intersection of layers B and C, and is dependency B-07 to the Replacement Programme.

Definitions

TermMeaning here
Protected fieldA BLP data element that feeds a regulatory return, a statutory or audited figure, or a SteerCo-level MI measure. Its definition, capture and timing may not change without the owner’s sign-off
Capture pointThe process step (map · step ID) at which a field is first populated or materially changed
Manual adjustmentAny step between BLP extract and reported figure performed outside a controlled, documented transformation (spreadsheet correction, re-classification, exclusion list)
LineageSource field → transformation → report line, with owner and frequency
