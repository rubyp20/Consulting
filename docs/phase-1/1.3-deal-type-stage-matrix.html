1. Purpose and method

The matrix answers one question: how much of the process actually differs by deal type? If the answer is “very little”, the to-be design can use a small number of journey patterns with deal-type parameters, and the Replacement Programme configures one workflow with rules rather than eleven. If the answer is “a lot”, the deal types stay. The matrix is the evidence either way.

Method

  1. Every L4 step from the 1.2 maps is listed once, grouped by the four source stages (Lead & Customer; Deal & Credit; Completion & Fulfilment; Post-approval and servicing events).
  2. Each live deal type (or the journey it uses) is a column. For each cell: ● the step occurs in the same form; ◆ it occurs with a variation (footnoted); — it does not occur.
  3. Variations are classified: P parameter (same step, different value: which product, which authority level, which items); F flow (different path or additional step); A actor (same step, different role).
  4. Commonality per stage = steps marked ● in all columns where the stage applies ÷ total steps in the stage.
  5. Deal types with no live customer process are excluded from the step matrix and handled in §5.

3.1 What this means for hypotheses B1–B4

HypothesisTestResult from the matrix (pre-validation)
B1 Shared deal startStage 1 core steps 100% commonSupported. 5 of 5 core steps identical across all ten columns. One “deal start” component with two entry variants (new customer; existing deal).
B2 Parameterised decision flowStage 2 ≥ 80% common across lending types; differences expressible as rulesSupported for lending types (82%). Every variant in NM/RN/EP/VA/SL/SR/SD is a parameter. CR and CS are a genuinely different flow and should be their own pattern.
B3 Redundant typesTypes with no live customer processSupported. Staged Drawdown (0 deals; superseded by Term Lending tranches), ESR Cleanse (records transfer), DO NOT USE. Plus two un-typed journeys (RF, SD allocation) that are being recorded under other types.
B4 Controls as rulesType-specific behaviours capturable as rules without residual flow differencePartly supported. EPR caps, RN definitional constraints, SR/SD routing, restructure flag all express as rules. Residual flow differences: CR/CS (second authority), RF (release sequence), RN (no security). These are three pattern-level differences, not eleven.
