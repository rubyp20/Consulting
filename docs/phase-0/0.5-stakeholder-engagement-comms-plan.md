## 4. Key messages

**From the Sponsor (kick-off and cascades)**
- We are replacing BLP. This project makes sure we replace the process too, not just the screens.
- The focus is renewals and variations, two-thirds of what we do, where colleagues spend most time on steps that add no value to the customer or to credit quality.
- We will quantify capacity we can free up. What we do with it is a management decision after the design is approved, and it will be discussed openly.
- Credit control will not be weakened. Every control we change will be reviewed and signed off by Credit Risk and Risk.
- Your input in workshops and the one-week activity survey is how the new process gets designed around real work, not assumptions.

**For Credit**
- Fast-track is a routing decision you set the criteria for, with the data in front of you. It moves low-risk renewals out of your queue so you spend time on the cases that need judgement.

**For the Replacement Programme**
- You get a requirements pack in your backlog format by Week 13, with high-impact decisions released by Week 10.
