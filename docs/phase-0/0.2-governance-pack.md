## 2. Steering Committee – Terms of Reference

**Purpose.** Own the project outcome. Approve gates, scope changes, the baseline, the design and the business case. Resolve escalations that cross functional lines. Release funding.

**Membership**

| Role | Member | Voting |
|---|---|---|
| Chair | MD, Business Banking (Executive Sponsor) | Yes |
| Business Owner | Head of Lending Operations | Yes |
| Credit | Chief Credit Officer or Head of Credit Risk Policy | Yes |
| Risk | Head of Operational Risk (second line) | Yes |
| Technology | Replacement Programme Director | Yes |
| Finance | Business Banking Finance Director | Yes |
| Front line | Head of Business Direct; Head of Strategic Finance | Yes |
| Partner | Engagement Lead | Advisory |
| Secretary | PMO | – |
| Attending | Engagement Manager, Bank PM | – |

**Cadence.** Fortnightly, 60 min, starting Week 2. Gate meetings extended to 90 min. Quorum: Chair (or delegate), Business Owner, Credit, Risk, Technology.

**Decision rights**
- Approve gates G0–G3 and scale/no-scale decisions in Phase 4
- Approve scope changes and change requests
- Endorse the Phase 1 baseline as the reference for benefits
- Approve the business case and release funding
- Resolve escalations not resolved at Design Authority

**Standing agenda.** Decisions required · Gate status · Progress vs plan (RAG by workstream) · Top risks and issues · Replacement Programme dependency status · Benefits status (from Phase 2) · AOB.

**Inputs.** SteerCo pack issued 2 working days before. Decision papers use the one-page format: decision required, options, recommendation, impact, owner.

**Outputs.** Decisions recorded in decision log within 1 working day; actions in action log.

## 3. Design Authority – Terms of Reference

**Purpose.** Approve the design: principles, journey patterns, to-be maps, routing criteria, control equivalence, data design and the requirements pack. Ensure the design is deliverable on the replacement platform and acceptable to Credit Risk, second line and MI.

**Membership**

| Role | Member | Sign-off responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Chair | Head of Lending Operations | Overall design |
| Credit Risk Policy | Head of Credit Risk Policy | Routing criteria; policy application |
| Second-line Risk | Senior Operational Risk Manager | Control equivalence |
| MI and Regulatory Reporting | Head of MI & Reg Reporting | Data lineage; protected fields |
| Replacement Programme | Solution Architect | Fit-gap; requirements pack deliverability |
| Front line | Senior RM (Strategic Finance); Business Direct Team Leader | Workability |
| Operations | Head of Lending Services; Head of Fulfilment | Workability |
| Partner | BPR/TOM Lead; Credit Risk and Controls Specialist | Presenting |
| Secretary | PMO | – |

**Cadence.** Fortnightly in Phase 1 (from Week 4); weekly in Phase 2; fortnightly in Phase 3; per release in Phase 4. 90 min.

**Decision rights.** Approve design artefacts listed above. Cannot change scope or objectives (SteerCo). Cannot waive a control without second-line signature. Disagreements not resolved in two sittings escalate to SteerCo.

**Rule for control changes.** No to-be map is approved while it contains an eliminated or automated control without a corresponding entry in the control equivalence register signed by second line.

## 5. Project leadership

Engagement Manager and Bank PM meet daily (15 min stand-up) and weekly (60 min plan and RAID review). Workstream leads attend the weekly. Weekly status report issued Friday to Sponsor, Business Owner and Replacement Programme Director.

## 6. Replacement Programme interface

Joint fortnightly 45-min session: Engagement Manager, Platform/Integration Analyst, Programme Manager, Solution Architect. Owns the joint decision log (0.8), the freeze date, and the interim design-decision releases. Replacement Programme Director sits on SteerCo; Solution Architect sits on Design Authority.

## 7. Escalation path

| Level | Forum | Resolution time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Working group | Within the meeting |
| 2 | Engagement Manager and Bank PM | 2 working days |
| 3 | Design Authority (design) / Project Leadership (delivery) | Next sitting |
| 4 | SteerCo | Next sitting or extraordinary |
| 5 | Executive Sponsor | 2 working days |
