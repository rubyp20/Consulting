## 1. Why this document exists

The target-state design is only valuable if the Replacement Programme can still act on it. This document records the Programme's schedule constraints, what each party needs from the other and by when, and every joint decision. It is reviewed at the fortnightly joint session and its status is a standing SteerCo item.

## 6. Joint session cadence

Fortnightly, 45 min, from Week 1: Engagement Manager, Platform/Integration Analyst, Programme PM, Solution Architect, Bank PM. Standing agenda: dependency status (§3), decisions due (§4), joint log (§5), risks shared with RAID (R-01, R-09, R-12).

## 7. Escalation

Unresolved dependency or decision after two joint sessions escalates to SteerCo, where the Programme Director and Sponsor both sit.
