## 1. Purpose

The hypothesis tree states what we believe is wrong and why, before discovery starts, so that Phase 1 collects evidence to prove or disprove specific claims rather than describing everything. Each leaf hypothesis has the evidence that would confirm it, the analysis that produces that evidence, the data source, the owner and the week.

Hypotheses are written to be falsifiable. "Renewals take too long" is not a hypothesis; "a material share of renewals could be decided within delegated authority without Credit review, with no increase in risk" is.

## 2. Governing question

**Can Business Bank's lending lifecycle be redesigned so that time to decision and cost to serve fall materially, with credit control maintained, in a form the replacement platform can deliver as standard?**

## 3. Tree

```
Governing question
│
├── A. Volume is concentrated in servicing journeys that are over-processed
│   ├── A1  Renewals follow the full credit flow despite no structural change
│   ├── A2  A material share of renewals meets objective low-risk criteria
│   ├── A3  EPR is used as a holding pattern, creating chains of extensions
│   └── A4  Variations are heterogeneous; a minority need full credit review
│
├── B. The journeys are structurally the same; the deal-type model multiplies them
│   ├── B1  Lead & Customer stage is identical across all journeys
│   ├── B2  Deal & Credit stage differs only by parameters, not by flow
│   ├── B3  Several deal types are redundant or non-customer (ESR Cleanse, DO NOT USE, Staged Drawdown orig.)
│   └── B4  Deal types carry their controls implicitly; controls can be made explicit as rules
│
├── C. Rework and wait, not touch time, dominate cycle time
│   ├── C1  "Deferred – further information required" is a high-frequency loop
│   ├── C2  Facility letter issue-and-return is the longest wait and a reset trigger
│   ├── C3  Changes after credit approval cause re-processing
│   └── C4  Hand-offs to Lending Services and Fulfilment carry queue time
│
├── D. Workarounds mask process and system gaps
│   ├── D1  Each bot and batch job substitutes for a missing process rule or system feature
│   ├── D2  Off-platform documents (Shadow Limit, Staged Drawdown letters) create control and data gaps
│   └── D3  Manual email issuance of letters creates audit and tracking gaps
│
├── E. Controls are a mix of policy, habit and system limitation
│   ├── E1  A share of control steps has no policy or regulatory source
│   ├── E2  Delegated-authority checks are performed manually where data exists to automate them
│   └── E3  Removing or automating controls is acceptable to second line if equivalence is shown
│
└── F. Data captured for reporting is at risk in any redesign unless mapped
    ├── F1  BLP fields feeding regulatory returns are not documented end to end
    └── F2  Operational MI relies on manual adjustment because capture is inconsistent
```

## 5. Exclusions and open questions

- We hold **no hypothesis on deposits** beyond location (SD-03); volume is too low to drive design.
- **Open:** whether the Replacement platform's standard process includes risk-based routing natively. Determines whether A2 becomes a configuration or a custom build. Resolved via D-07 by W8.
- **Open:** whether Corporate journeys differ structurally. Tested via proxy in WS-14.

## 6. How the tree is used

- Phase 1 workshops and analyses are planned against this register, not the other way round.
- Each hypothesis is marked Confirmed / Partly confirmed / Rejected in the current-state report (1.11) with the evidence.
- Rejected hypotheses are reported, not dropped; they are part of the fact base.
- The register is frozen at G0; new hypotheses raised in Phase 1 are added as "emergent" and reported separately.
