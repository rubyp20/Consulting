How to read this document

Legend

Structure

Section 1 maps the three shared components (SC-1a, SC-1b, SC-2, SC-3), which carry most of the design. Section 2 maps the pattern-specific segments (P3 removal, P5) and the four exception paths that are now first-class. Section 3 shows each pattern as a composition. Section 4 is the disposition register: every one of the 62 as-is step rows from 1.3, marked Eliminated, Automated, Consolidated or Retained, with the to-be location, the reason, the principle applied and the control effect. Section 5 lists the design decision records and the equivalence and policy items the maps depend on.

Conventions

Same notation as 1.2. Grey boxes are platform (automated) steps; Cn badges are controls from 1.6, retained or relocated; new controls are named in notes. Where a step is labelled with an RS-nn reference, its behaviour is set by that rules table in 2.4 rather than by the map.

What changes, in numbers

DispositionAs-is step rowsMeaning
Eliminated5Work stops; the need is removed by design
Automated32The platform performs it, with evidence
Consolidated2Merged with another step or relocated
Retained23Still a human step, usually with a rule or evidence added

Of the 62 rows, 37 (60%) are eliminated or automated. No regulatory control is eliminated; four controls are relocated or converted to reconciliations; nine are strengthened.
