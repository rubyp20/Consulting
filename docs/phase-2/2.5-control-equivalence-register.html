1. How the register works

1.1 Change classes

Each of the 28 controls from 1.6 is assigned one class. Only two classes require a formal equivalence judgement; the others require second line to confirm the classification.

CodeClassMeaningSecond-line action
RTRetainedSame control, same mechanism, same evidence; possibly relocated within a component without changing timingConfirm classification
STStrengthenedSame objective; mechanism changes from manual or undocumented to system-applied with recorded evidence; the to-be is demonstrably at least as effectiveConfirm that the new evidence is sufficient
AUAutomated, like for likeSame check, performed by the platform instead of a person, producing equivalent evidenceConfirm evidence equivalence; agree exception handling
RLRelocatedControl moves earlier or later in the flow; same mechanism; timing change must not weaken itJudge the timing change
EQReplaced with equivalenceThe as-is control is bypassed or removed for a defined population; a different control or set of controls provides equivalent assuranceFormal equivalence judgement (section 3)
PQPolicy questionThe change is not an equivalence question but a change to what policy permits; routed to Credit Risk Policy; as-is control retained until answeredNote; sign only after policy decision

1.2 Second-line acceptance criteria (from the Week 2 interview; to be confirmed in writing by second line)

An EQ entry is accepted when all of the following are shown:

  1. Objective preserved. The risk the as-is control addressed is named and the replacement addresses the same risk for the same population.
  2. Evidence at least as good. The to-be produces evidence that is recorded, attributable, timestamped and reportable; manual evidence is not replaced by weaker system evidence.
  3. Population bounded. The deals to which the replacement applies are defined by rule on system-held data; exclusions are explicit; anything outside the population follows the as-is control.
  4. Detective control present. Where a preventive control is relaxed, a detective control (sampling, cohort MI, exception reporting) with a defined rate, owner and escalation is in place from day one.
  5. Reversible. The replacement can be suspended for a population or segment without system change if findings warrant it.
  6. Regulatory untouched. No regulatory control’s capture, timing or definition is changed, and protected fields (1.8 §6) are preserved.

3. Equivalence entries · formal judgements

CE-001 · Independent Credit assessment (C6) bypassed for RS-01-eligible renewalsEQ · DDR-001 · linked PQ-001 · Status: Reviewed
Risk addressed by as-isA renewal is approved without an independent judgement that the credit remains acceptable; deterioration in the customer, sector or security is missed at the review point.
PopulationRenewals where every RS-01 condition holds at evaluation: no change to any term; grade within band and not stale; no downgrade in 12 months; covenants compliant; no RS-11 flag; no arrears; no EPR in 12 months; not excluded by sector / product; exposure within ceiling; at most two consecutive fast-tracks. Everything else follows R-FULL with the failing condition recorded.
Replacement controlsPreventive: (a) RS-01 evaluated by platform on system-held data, result and inputs recorded; (b) structural-change validation blocks any term change from P2; (c) automated DA determination (RS-05) with recorded authority level; (d) RS-04 reduced items enforced, including current grade and covenant status; (e) RS-01 c.12 forces a full review at least every third cycle; (f) RS-11 flag excludes any connection with an indicator. Detective: (g) Credit post-approval sampling of R-FAST approvals at 10% initially, risk-weighted, floor 5%, with findings categorised and fed to thresholds; (h) quarterly cohort MI comparing 12-month downgrade, arrears and referral rates for fast-track vs. full-review renewals; (i) segment suspension trigger at a 5% finding rate.
EvidencePer deal: rule version, each condition’s input and result, route, approver and level, items present. Per month: sampling results. Per quarter: cohort outcome MI to Head of Credit and Risk Committee.
Residual riskDeterioration between grade reviews not captured by RS-11 indicators (e.g. sector shock without arrears). Mitigation: grade age ≤ 15 months (c.4); downgrade check (c.5); sampling weighted to band-edge grades; Credit Risk may add sector overlays to RS-11 at any time without system change.
Acceptance criteria test1 objective preserved ✓ (same risk, defined population) · 2 evidence ✓ (recorded, attributable) · 3 population bounded ✓ (rule on system data; RM may only force R-FULL) · 4 detective ✓ (sampling, cohort MI, suspension) · 5 reversible ✓ (suspension by segment is a rule-table change) · 6 regulatory ✓ (F06 grade capture unchanged; refresh rule added).
DependenciesPQ-001 (authority): if refused, this entry is withdrawn and R-FAST retains Credit assessment with a reduced submission. PQ-004 (thresholds). A2 cohort evidence at G1.
Second-line conditions (to record)[Sampling rate and weighting; cohort MI format; suspension trigger; grade-age maximum; review after two quarters]
DecisionReviewed W9; decision at W11 sitting after PQ-001
CE-002 · Three-month unreturned-letter reset replaced by graduated staleness controlEQ · DDR-005 · Status: Reviewed
Risk addressed by as-isA facility is documented and fulfilled on an approval whose basis has changed in the interval (customer position, security, terms), because too long has elapsed since the decision.
PopulationAll deals in SC-3 awaiting a returned letter; any pattern.
Replacement controlsPreventive: (a) automated chase at 2, 4, 8 weeks with timestamps; (b) RM extension at 10 weeks with reason code, visible to line; (c) at 3 months, platform re-validation of the facts the approval depended on: grade unchanged or improved; no RS-11 flag; no arrears; terms, security, parties unchanged; any failure resets to SC-2; (d) reset at 6 months regardless; (e) material change at any time (RS-12) resets. Detective: (f) monthly MI on letters outstanding > 8 weeks, extensions granted, re-validations passed / failed, resets.
EvidenceChase log; extension reason; re-validation record with each fact’s value at 3 months; reset reason.
Residual riskA change not captured by the re-validation items. Mitigation: item list owned by Credit Risk Policy and extendable; 6-month hard stop; the as-is control had no re-validation at all, only a reset, so the to-be examines more, not less.

DependenciesPlatform timers and status (G1 decision 3); RS-08 item list agreed.
Second-line conditions (to record)[Re-validation item list; whether 6 months may be shortened for New Money]
DecisionReviewed W9; decision at W11
CE-003 · Independent Credit assessment (C6) bypassed for administrative variationsEQ · DDR-009 · Status: Reviewed
Risk addressed by as-isA change that affects credit risk is made without Credit seeing it, because it was presented as administrative.
PopulationVariations whose reason codes are all in the RS-03 R-FAST set (V-01, V-02, V-10; V-05 subject to PQ-003) and no RS-11 flag.
Replacement controlsPreventive: (a) reason code mandatory at SC-1a; (b) most-material-wins combination rule: any R-FULL reason forces R-FULL; (c) V-12 “other” always R-FULL; (d) line approval with recorded reason and authority (RS-05, RS-13); (e) letter, security and fulfilment gates set by reason so an administrative code cannot produce a term change in documents. Detective: (f) Credit sampling weighted to V-02 and V-10; (g) exception report of any administrative variation that changed a monitored field (limit, rate, maturity, security, party) in the same period; (h) segment suspension as CE-001.
EvidenceReason codes; route; approver and level; field-change log vs. reason.
Residual riskMis-coding to obtain the fast route. Mitigation: field-change exception report (g) detects it mechanically; sampling targets the codes most open to misuse; repeat findings by individual escalated through line.
