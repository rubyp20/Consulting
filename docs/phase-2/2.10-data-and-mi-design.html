5.3 Exposure update rule (SD-01 option c; DAT-04)

ESR Cleanse records update personal exposure on personal-associated deals and external NAG exposure on business records. The migration specification (WS3 to Programme Data Lead, W7) states, for each exposure field: source record, derivation, timing, and the equivalent derivation in the to-be data model so that the update happens by rule rather than by a deal. Post-migration exposure per connection is recalculated and compared with the pre-migration value; variances above a tolerance set by Credit Risk are investigated before go-live.
