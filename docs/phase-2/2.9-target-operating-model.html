3. Role profiles · what changes

Relationship Manager / Business Direct adviserGPe-01, GPe-02 · highest change intensity
Purpose (to-be)Own the customer relationship and the quality of the submission; decide scope; act on system prompts; approve within held authority where RS-13 assigns it.
Stops doingNavigating to find customers and deals; issuing letters by email and tracking in mailboxes; chasing Credit, LS and Fulfilment by message; reading the DA matrix; re-keying analytics; recording ESR Cleanse migrations as deals; deciding whether a signature is needed; instructing security by email.
Starts doingConfirming scope and reason codes at deal start; responding to early-warning prompts; preparing to a stated submission standard; responding to structured requests; extending stale letters with reason; evidencing CPs in the single view; (SF, per RS-13) approving eligible renewals and administrative variations within authority with system evidence.
Keeps doingCustomer analysis and credit memo on R-FULL; customer contact; decline communication; relationship time.
Accountability changeFast-route approvals (where RS-13 assigns to RM) carry personal accountability supported by rule evidence and sampling. Addressed by clear criteria (RS-01, RS-03), evidence attached automatically, training, and sampling feedback rather than blame (DB-06).
SkillsSubmission standard; reason taxonomy; reading rule evidence; new platform. Less: system navigation; document admin.
MeasuresSubmission first-time-right (R1); scope accuracy (sampling findings on coding); T7 renewal lateness; customer relationship time (survey N01).
Capacity shift (illustrative)≈ 23 FTE-eq released across RM / BD / Bus Ops (1.10 §4.1), mainly from letters (OP-15), deal start (OP-14), fast-track preparation (OP-01) and manual DA (OP-06); intended destination: customer time and lead handling, per SD-04.
Line manager / Business Direct team leaderGPe-02 · new accountability
Purpose (to-be)Approve fast-route deals within line authority (BD team leaders; SF and Corporate line per RS-13); own stage SLA for deal start and completion in their team; act on escalations.
Starts doingR-FAST approvals with evidence review (not re-assessment); extension approvals at week 10 visibility; queue management against SLAs; acting on sampling findings for their team.
Accountability changeBecomes the authority holder for the fast route in volume segments. Requires: held authority in platform profile; training on RS-01 / RS-03 evidence; sampling MI by team.
MeasuresTeam SLA attainment (S1, S3); sampling finding rate; approvals within authority (100% by construction).
Credit assessor and Credit managerGPe-03, GC-05 · caseload and function change
Purpose (to-be)Independent assessment of R-FULL deals; detective assurance over fast routes; escalation decisions; co-ownership of rule thresholds with Credit Risk Policy.
Stops doingAssessing eligible unchanged renewals and administrative variations (subject to PQ-001 / CE-001 / CE-003); re-reading resubmissions from scratch; free-text deferral requests; distress screening as the first point of detection.
Starts doingSampling review of fast-route approvals (new function, CE-001 §6.3); structured deferral requests; escalation decisions at loop limit; quarterly cohort MI review; feeding sampling findings into RS-01 / RS-03 / RS-04 maintenance; parallel referral review (PQ-002).
Keeps doingFull assessment and decision on R-FULL; distress judgement; appeal review (independent assessor).
Dis-benefitDB-01: average case complexity rises as simple cases leave the queue; assessment time per case may increase. Netted in 2.13 against capacity released. Role profile and grading reviewed by Head of Credit.
Sampling functionIllustrative: 10% of ≈ 7,100 eligible renewals + ≈ 4,400 administrative variations ≈ 1,150 samples / yr ≈ 0.5–0.7 FTE-eq at 45–60 min each; rate reviewed quarterly, floor 5%. Reviewer must be independent of the segment’s line approvals.
MeasuresS2a queue SLA; R1 first-time-right (shared with RMs); sampling completion and finding rate; cohort outcome parity (fast vs. full).
Capacity shift (illustrative)≈ 5 FTE-eq released (OP-01, OP-02, OP-17) less ≈ 0.6 FTE-eq sampling and the DB-01 complexity effect (to measure).
Lending Services / Securities · Fulfilment · Business Operations / Corporate AssociateGPe-04, GPe-06
Lending ServicesWorks a queue with SLA; instructions arrive from approval conditions (no transcription); registration status recorded with evidence; no instruction on unchanged security; removal release before system release. 5.1 Design Authority in run mode

After G3 the Design Authority continues at reduced cadence (quarterly) as the forum that approves pattern, rule-ownership and SLA changes, reviews sampling and cohort MI with second line, and decides on fast-route extensions (e.g. P1 Increase fast-track, Release 2). Membership unchanged; chaired by Head of Lending Operations.

6.2 Spans, layers and structure

  • No change to reporting lines is required by the design. Accountabilities are added to existing roles (fast-route approval to line; sampling to Credit; rule ownership to Credit Risk Policy; SLA ownership to heads of function). Structure change, if any, follows the SD-04 decision and is for line management.
  • One new function: fast-route sampling within Credit (≈ 0.5–0.7 FTE-eq illustrative), independent of segment line approvals. May be a rotation rather than a post.
  • One accountability made explicit: end-to-end cycle time sits with the Head of Lending Operations, who already chairs the Design Authority.
  • Segment variation reduced to RS-13. Corporate retains line-only fast-route approval and Corporate Associate checks under C27.
