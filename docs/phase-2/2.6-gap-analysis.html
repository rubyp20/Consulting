1. Method and reading guide

A gap is the difference between how something works today and how the target design needs it to work. Each gap is stated as a pair (as-is, to-be), sized for closure difficulty, assigned to the thing that closes it (a deliverable, an opportunity, a decision or a Programme item), given an owner, and placed in a wave. The point of the document is not to list differences but to make sure every difference has a closer; a gap with no closer is a design that will not land.

3.2 What the register shows

  • Nine hard gaps, and they cluster. Three are the renewal fast-track seen from three dimensions (GP-03, GPo-01, GC-01): one policy decision closes all three. Three are integrations the Programme owns (GT-06, GT-08, GT-09), none of which the Release 1 design depends on. One is document capability (GT-03 / GP-07), which the largest cycle-time opportunity depends on and which G1 decision 3 addresses. One is early-warning data (GD-04), mitigated by conservative thresholds. That is the whole hard list.
  • Most of the technology column is expected to be standard. Timers, queues, SSO, pattern configuration and reporting (GT-01, 02, 05, 11, 12) are ordinary lending-platform capability; 2.7 confirms. The design is deliberately built so that the two capabilities most likely to be weak (rules depth GT-04; documents GT-03) degrade gracefully: rules can be shallower with manual evidence; documents can start with generation and tracking before e-signature.
  • The people gaps are about accountability, not headcount. GPe-02 (line accountability for fast-track), GPe-03 (Credit role shifts to complex cases and sampling) and GPe-05 (someone owns end-to-end cycle time) are the three that determine whether the design works in practice. They are the core of 2.9.
  • Four gaps can close now. GP-11, GPe-08, GD-01, GD-05 need no platform: ESR Cleanse out of the deal model, data ownership, lineage and the migration spec for exposure. Together with OP-29 to OP-31 they are Wave 0.
  • Nothing in the data column removes a regulatory capture point. GD-05 and GD-06 are preservation gaps: they exist to make sure migration and cut-over carry the fields the as-is captures.

3.3 Gaps with no closer yet

Every gap above has a closer. Two closers are outside the project’s control and are tracked as RAID items: PQ-001 (R-03, R-10) and document capability (R-09). If either fails, the fallback design is stated on the gap row and in 2.3 §5.2.
