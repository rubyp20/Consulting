## Module 5 — Quiz answers

**1.** Business-led digital roadmap; talent; operating model; technology; data; adoption and scaling [PUBLIC — Lamarre, Smaje, Zemmel, "Rewired", 2023]. KYB depends most on data (entity, ownership and document data sit in several systems and must become governed data products before any agent can reason over them) and on adoption and scaling (onboarding is run by operations teams whose workflow, controls and second-line sign-off must change for benefits to appear).
Manager-level benchmark: names all six without hesitation, picks two with a one-line reason each, and notes that governance (model risk) is a close third for a regulated process.

**2.** Diagnosis: the budget is inverted against the 10-20-70 heuristic [PUBLIC — BCG, 2023–24]; only 15 percent sits in the 70 percent category, so the programme is shaped to produce pilots that cannot scale. Most important reallocation: move spend from licences and platform into process redesign, controls engagement and front-line change, targeting change at 50 to 60 percent of total.
Manager-level benchmark: states the diagnosis in one sentence, names the reallocation, and adds the caveat that 10-20-70 is a heuristic and that the exact split should be derived from the specific domains.

**3.** Deploy: adopt off-the-shelf tools for broad productivity, e.g. an enterprise GenAI assistant for all staff. Reshape: redesign an end-to-end process around AI, e.g. agentic dispute resolution with human exception handling. Invent: new propositions, e.g. an AI-native embedded-finance credit product for SME platforms [PUBLIC — BCG framing, 2023–24, VERIFY wording].
Manager-level benchmark: adds that measurable P&L sits mostly in Reshape and that portfolio shape should be checked against maturity.

**4.** "Our diagnostic shows the platform is already ahead of the organisation: technology scores 3, adoption scores 1 and data scores 2. I would put the next EUR 12 million into data products for the two priority domains and into a funded change and adoption capability, and revisit the platform upgrade once production deployments are being throttled by the platform rather than by the organisation."
Manager-level benchmark: references the evidence, redirects rather than refuses, and states the trigger that would change the answer.

**5.** Defend: 8 to 12 percent of EUR 400 million = EUR 32 to 48 million a year. Transform: 35 percent of EUR 90 million = EUR 31.5 million, plus 8 percent of the remaining EUR 310 million = EUR 24.8 million, total EUR 56.3 million a year (a range of roughly EUR 50 to 62 million if the "elsewhere" rate is flexed between 6 and 10 percent).
Manager-level benchmark: shows the arithmetic, labels the result as run-rate at maturity rather than year-one value, and notes that a ramp and a risk adjustment (Module 9) must be applied before the figure goes to a CFO.
