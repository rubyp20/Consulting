## Module 6 — Quiz answers

**1.** L3 tasks have a definable input, output, decision logic and error profile, which is what an AI pattern acts on; L2 stages are where benefits appear because a stage only gets faster or cheaper when its whole set of tasks changes. Example L3: "build ownership structure and identify UBOs" (fit assessed here). Example L2: "entity verification" (business case built here, covering registry lookup, extraction, UBO unwinding, ID and V and reconciliation together).
Manager-level benchmark: adds that assessing fit at L2 hides judgement tasks inside automatable stages, and that building cases at L3 produces unbankable micro-benefits.

**2.** Volume, effort, error and rework, delay, customer or colleague friction. Weight Delay most heavily, because it measures each task's share of elapsed cycle time including waiting, which is the direct driver of the ambition; Effort would be weighted for a cost ambition.
Manager-level benchmark: notes that heat is not fit and that weighting should be agreed with the sponsor before scoring, not adjusted afterwards.

**3.** Task 2.2 (chasing) has the higher fit at high autonomy. It is high volume, low judgement, bounded by policy (what to chase, how often, when to escalate), reversible (a wrong chase is a nuisance, not a regulatory breach), and acts through tools the agent can be given (case system, email, portal). Checker review (6.1) is a control with regulatory accountability; a copilot can flag inconsistencies but the approval decision stays with a human.
Manager-level benchmark: frames the reason in terms of error tolerance and accountability, and proposes the copilot pattern for 6.1 rather than dismissing it.

**4.** Example: "Agent that classifies and extracts incoming KYB documents, checks completeness and expiry against the jurisdiction-specific requirements matrix, and returns a completed document pack to the analyst with exceptions flagged." Baseline: documents received a year, handle time per document, first-time completeness rate, average days to complete the pack, rework rate from the checker on document issues.
Manager-level benchmark: the rewrite names a pattern, a task and an outcome, specifies the autonomy option, and the baseline list is specific enough to be pulled from the case system.

**5.** False positives: 48,000 x 0.94 = 45,120. Time saved per false positive: 9 - 3 = 6 minutes. Total saved: 45,120 x 6 = 270,720 minutes = 4,512 hours. FTE: 4,512 / 1,600 = 2.8 FTE.
Manager-level benchmark: shows the arithmetic, then adds that the saving is capacity rather than cost until the roles are redeployed or removed, and that the true-positive handle time may rise slightly because analysts spend more time on the cases that matter.
