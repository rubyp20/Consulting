# Agentic and generative AI across the payments value chain

**[Open the PDF (48 pages)](Agentic-GenAI-Payments-Value-Chain-Bank-X.pdf)**

This is an illustrative response to an RFP. In it, a payments consulting practice lead answers an Indian bank's question: where should generative and agentic AI be used across the payments value chain, and in what order? The worked example uses **Bank X**, a fictional large Indian private-sector bank.

## The assessment framework: five lenses, applied in order

| Lens | Question | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Where to play | Which of 10 value-chain stages × 5 business lines hold cost, loss and revenue pools? | Value-pool heatmap, sized activity by activity |
| 2 · What kind of AI | Which of six archetypes fits? (predictive ML, copilot, document AI, conversational, agentic workflow, agentic commerce) | Archetype per use case |
| 3 · How much autonomy | What autonomy cap (L0–L4) follows from error cost × reversibility, plus any RBI or NPCI override? | Autonomy cap and controls |
| 4 · Is it worth it | Does it pass six risk gates (data residency, DPDP, customer harm, model risk, outsourcing, scheme rules)? If so, score value, feasibility and fit | Priority index = 0.45 V + 0.35 F + 0.20 S |
| 5 · When and how | What balance of deploy, reshape and invent? What dependencies and waves? | 24-month roadmap and business case |

## What is inside

- **Brief and point of view:** a playback of the RFP and an issue tree. Indian market context: UPI volumes, the new UPI merchant discount rate (MDR), RBI's DPIP fraud registry and FREE-AI framework, and the RBI draft model-risk guidance. Also covers the six AI archetypes, the autonomy ladder and five hypotheses.
- **Worked example for Bank X:**
  - 50 use cases, of which 21 pass the risk gates and are scored and 10 are prioritised.
  - A value-feasibility matrix and portfolio mix.
  - About ₹465 crore a year of value at stake by Year 3 (range ₹350–580 crore).
- **Deep dives:**
  - Dispute and chargeback agent.
  - AML triage and scam interdiction.
  - Reconciliation (with a break taxonomy).
  - Vernacular servicing.
  - Agentic commerce on UPI.
- **Roadmap and enablers:**
  - Three waves with gate criteria.
  - A business case with breakeven in month 17.
  - An India-only target architecture, a hub-and-spoke operating model, FREE-AI governance, KPIs and risks.
- **Proposed engagement:**
  - A 12-week diagnostic with two sandbox pilots and a day-by-day first week.
  - A data request, a fee build (days × rate, three options) and an RFP compliance matrix.
  - A simulated red-team panel.

## How to read the numbers

Every figure carries a label:

- **P:** a public source, cited on the sources page.
- **A:** assumed for illustration.
- **C:** calculated from P and A.

Bank X, its data, the RFP and the panel scores are fictional. Market and regulatory facts were gathered on 13 October 2026 from search summaries. Verify them against the primary RBI, NPCI and MeitY releases before relying on them. The document is not produced by, or affiliated with, any named consulting firm.
